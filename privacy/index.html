Privacy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

BK Video Edit is a trading name of Banuka Kannangara (Pvt) Ltd, Sri Lanka. This page explains what we do with the details and files you send through this website.

What we collect

  • The details you type into the project form: your name, business name, email, country, optional WhatsApp number and your project brief.
  • The files you upload, such as video, audio, images and documents.
  • Your browser's timezone, so we know when it is reasonable to contact you.

This website uses the Meta Pixel, a tool from Meta (Facebook and Instagram). It records page visits and when a project form is sent, so we can measure our adverts and show them to relevant people. Meta may use cookies for this. You can control ad personalization in your Facebook or Instagram ad settings.

The sample videos on the home page are embedded from YouTube, Instagram and Facebook, and those services may set their own cookies when the videos load.

How we use it

Only to quote, edit and deliver your project, and to contact you about it. We do not sell your information or use your footage for anything else without your permission.

Where it is stored

Uploaded files are stored in our Google Drive. Project details are stored in Google Drive and in ClickUp, our project management tool. Both are provided by companies based in the United States, so your information may be processed outside your country.

How long we keep it

We keep project files and details for as long as needed to complete the work and handle follow-up requests. You can ask us to delete them sooner.

Your choices

You can ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it and your files. Email banuka@bkauthoritymedia.com and we will act on your request.

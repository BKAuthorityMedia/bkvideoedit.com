[ Questions ]

Before you send.

How do I send large files?

The upload page takes files up to 1 GB each and 10 GB per project, straight from your browser. If your footage is already on Google Drive, Dropbox or Frame.io, paste the link instead.

Can you edit in a specific style?

Yes. Paste links to videos you like in the upload form and we will match that style for your brand, including pacing, captions, transitions and graphics.

Can I see reviews from past clients?

Yes. Our client reviews and past projects are public on our Upwork profile.

How fast is delivery?

Short-form edits are delivered within one day of you approving the quote. Podcast episodes and larger projects get a delivery date with the quote.

When do I pay?

Nothing is charged for sending footage or getting a quote. Payment details and terms come with your quote, and work starts once you approve it.

Is my footage kept private?

Yes. Files go directly into our private storage and are only opened by our editing team. We do not publish client work without permission.

Where are you based?

Our editors are in Sri Lanka (GMT+5:30) and work with clients worldwide in English. Send footage at the end of your day and it is in our queue by our morning.

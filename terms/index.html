3. Turnaround times

24-hour turnaround. Our 24-hour turnaround applies only to eligible projects, and the 24 hours are counted from the moment the project is accepted (section 2), not from the moment you upload.

What is delivered in 24 hours. The 24-hour turnaround is for the first delivery: the first full version of your edited video, sent to you for review. It is not the time for the final, completed video. After the first delivery you review it and send any changes, we make the revisions included in your quote, and the video is final once you approve it. The time this takes depends on how quickly feedback is sent and how many changes are needed, so it is in addition to the 24 hours. The final video can therefore take more than one day once revisions are included.

A project is normally eligible when it is:

The delivery time in your quote is the one that applies to your project, and unless the quote says otherwise it means the time to the first delivery.
